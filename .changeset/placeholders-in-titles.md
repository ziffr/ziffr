A widget's title and the texts of a View Design can now use Placeholders such as {metric}, {latest} and {change yoy}, so a heading like "{metric}: {latest} ({change yoy})" stays true as the values move on. They are filled in on dashboards, in Snapshots, on View Links and on slides, and a Metric the viewer may not see stays empty. The Snapshot editor lists the Placeholders under the title.
