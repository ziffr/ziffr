View Designs can draw a Formula Tree: the formula of a Derived Metric as boxes, every box an exact number. The parts of a sum share the width of the box above them by their size (a part taken away is hatched and carries its minus sign); the factors of a product or a ratio are laid side by side, equal and marked not to scale. A part with no value is a gap, never a 0. It is drawn only from Metrics you may see: a formula with a part that is not shared with you is refused, without naming it and without an "other parts" box that would give its value away.
