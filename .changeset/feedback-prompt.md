Ziffr now asks signed-in people now and then whether they like it (hosted Ziffr only, until you switch it on elsewhere): a yes opens the repository on GitHub, where they can star it themselves, a no asks what is missing and keeps the words without a name. Self-hosters: it is off unless `ZIFFR_FEEDBACK_PROMPT=true` (or Installation settings, Feedback); an Installation Admin reads what people said on that page.
