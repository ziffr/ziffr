A short Setup Guide opens when you first join: about you, a starting point, how numbers come in and out, your first Metric, your team. Each step can be skipped, it stays one click away until you are done, and a Test Message and Ask me now show that Ziffr and you reach each other. It replaces the old "Get your workspace ready" list and the trial's own guide, on hosted and self-hosted Ziffr alike.
