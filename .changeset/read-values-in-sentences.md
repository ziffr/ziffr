Ziffr reads every number one way, wherever it comes in, and your Workspace's Number Style settles `1.500`. Answers can be sentences: "we had 2 signups last week" records 2 for last week, in English or German, and Ziffr asks back instead of guessing when an answer holds several numbers or names another period than the Prompt asked for.
