A Metric can now have a Retention: how long its values are kept, and whether older ones are condensed into longer periods or deleted. Its page shows in counts what a setting would do before you turn it on, and a confirmed Retention waits 7 days in which it can be cancelled; Owners can also set a default for new Metrics. Nothing is condensed or deleted yet.
