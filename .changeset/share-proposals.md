An assistant can now prepare sharing a dashboard with someone (the `propose_share` tool and "Share these numbers with…" Starter). Nothing is shared or sent until you confirm it in Ziffr, where you see what the recipient would see; assistants that support MCP Apps also show a preview in the chat. Connect an assistant with the new "Propose sharing" choice to use it.
