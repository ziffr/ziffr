Drivers and Correlation now say what they found as a Takeaway: one sentence in your Metrics' own words, a strength in four words (strong, moderate, weak, can't tell from chance), what it was compared on, and the line "This shows what has gone together, not what caused what." Before a result is shown Ziffr checks that the data suits the method: it refuses, in words and with no number, when there is too little to go on (a Metric that has hardly changed, too many periods without a value), and puts a plain line above the sentence when a few periods pull the result or the link changed. A result with such a line is never called strong. Show the numbers gives r, the pairs of periods and the basis. New guides: Reading a Takeaway and Methods.
