Owners and Admins see the Workspace's Size and usage under Workspace settings: how many Metrics it holds of how many its Workspace Limit allows. While it is over the limit they see a line on every page, and every Owner gets an email when the 14 days of room start and when they end. Near the limit, New metric shows how many Metrics the Workspace holds of how many. The Audit Log records archived and brought back Metrics and changes to the limit.
