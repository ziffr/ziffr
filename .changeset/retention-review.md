Retention is safer after its security review. What earlier runs took now counts against what you confirmed, so values that arrive later for periods already deleted cannot make the job delete more than you saw. Condensing checks that its Condensed Value is still there before it removes the originals, and a Correction still waiting for a decision holds its period. The download before a delete holds every Breakdown row, also replaced ones. A Retention the job holds back or stopped is confirmed again with **Confirm again…**, without turning it off first.
