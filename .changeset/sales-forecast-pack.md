A new Pack of Ziffr's own, **Sales forecast**, for the weekly forecast meeting: Commit, Best case, Closed this quarter, Pipeline value and Quota by week, with the gap to quota and pipeline coverage worked out, a dashboard, a designed deck for the meeting in three brand Themes (cover, the call against quota, the gap by salesperson and more, filled in from each week's figures) and a Workflow that asks on Monday, has the sales lead approve, locks the week and sends its Snapshot. It holds Themes, so an Owner installs it under Workspace → Packs.
