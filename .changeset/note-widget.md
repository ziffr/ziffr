Dashboards have a **Note widget**: your own words beside your numbers, with an optional heading, simple lists and a highlighted look. Free layouts are now offered only where Ziffr has a license key for the slide editor. Self-hosters: without a key, free layouts you already made are kept and say that they cannot be shown; add the key in the Installation Settings to see and change them again.
