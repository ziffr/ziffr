Analyze can now answer "What if this Metric changes?": if a Metric goes up or down by an amount or a percent, what has gone with it in the Metrics that move with it, and in the ones those move, in the Metrics' own units, up to two steps. What comes from a Derived Metric's formula is marked as worked out and has no range; what is read from what has gone together is marked as estimated and says "likely between", and a value beyond what the Metric has been is only a guess. It never reads as a promise or as a cause.
