Programs and assistants that read a Metric's values now see who entered a value for its submitter (`entered_by` and `entered_by_name`, read only; nothing for a Guest's key, nothing writes it). The Usage page lists who had values entered by others in the last 90 days, and the demo shows a Stand-in's value entered while the submitter was Away and one the submitter later replaced.
