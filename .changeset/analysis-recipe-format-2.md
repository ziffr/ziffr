Developers: an Analysis Recipe can now be written in format `ziffr.analysis/2`, which adds the newer Methods as steps (rank, partial and lagged correlation, the usual range, percentiles, a fit and its importance), tables as results, and a Takeaway as an output, in the Method's own words. Recipes in `ziffr.analysis/1` load exactly as before. The schema is `docs/reference/analysis-recipe-2.schema.json`, and Packs and View Designs accept either format.
