Installation Admins have Users, Workspaces and Health pages: block, unblock or sign out a User, give a running trial more days, and see whether messages and background jobs work. They see account and Workspace facts there (names, Owners, dates and counts), never what a Workspace holds. Self-hosters: `ZIFFR_ADMIN_URL` can give these pages an address of their own, which you can restrict at your network.
