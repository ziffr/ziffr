A Personal Webhook now hears as its person: the Tasks and Questions given to them or asked by them, in full, and the values of up to 50 Metrics they pick, each checked again at the moment it is sent, so nothing leaves that the person may not see. An unusual value carries its number only for those who may see a value waiting for Approval. No other person's email address is ever in a message.
