A Metric can now be handed over: its owner, an Owner or an Admin picks another Member on the Metric's page with "Hand over…". The new owner is told after their Quiet Hours, the previous owner stays on as Editor, and the Audit Log records it. The screen for removing someone offers an Owner to take over first, and a person who owns Metrics can't be made a Guest until they are handed over.
