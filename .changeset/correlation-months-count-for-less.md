A Metric that follows its own past (most monthly Metrics do) no longer reads as moving with another one so easily. How clearly two Metrics move together is now judged on how many separate months they are worth, so the strength word can be weaker, often "can't tell from chance", than before for slow-moving or seasonal Metrics. The Takeaway says when this changed the word, and Show the numbers holds the effective number of months.
