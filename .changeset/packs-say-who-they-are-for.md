Packs can say who they are for: an optional `audience` in the Pack file names the industries, kinds of customers, Business Functions, sizes and countries it suits, and Ziffr's own twelve Packs now do. Ziffr uses it only to suggest the best fits first, with the reason, and never hides a Pack. Developers: `@ziffr/pack` checks the field and exports the fixed lists.
