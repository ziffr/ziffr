Owners get one email a month when something in their Workspace is unused: how many dashboards, decks and Metrics nobody opened for 90 days, with a few names and a link to the Usage page, and never a value. It is on at first; switch it off under Your Deliveries or with the unsubscribe link in the email. Self-hosters: the Docker clock calls the new `usage-digest` job daily at 07:00 UTC; it needs working email.
