Dashboards have an **Add widget** button, and an empty card in the next free place while there are only a few widgets. Both open a panel that shows every way of drawing a number as cards, like the Gallery, drawn with the Metric you pick: drag a card onto the dashboard or press + (on a phone, tap it). It replaces the Pool on dashboards, a short dashboard title no longer breaks onto two lines, and an empty dashboard no longer opens in edit mode.
