**Use this pack** sets a Pack up one question at a time: see it with made-up numbers, name the people, press **Set it up**. Nothing is sent. It is on the Packs page, for Marketplace Packs and Pack files, and in the Setup Guide. A Pack with nothing to set up has **Add to my Workspace**. An installed Pack lets you open its dashboard, change who provides and approves the numbers, and **Choose parts yourself** for the detailed form. Export as Pack can now write set-up questions and try them.
