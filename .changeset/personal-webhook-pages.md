Personal Webhooks have their pages. Under You → Your Webhooks a person adds an address of their own, picks what it hears (their tasks and questions, the values of Metrics they pick), sends a test, replaces the secret, switches it off or deletes it, and reads its log. Under Workspace → Webhooks an Owner or Admin switches "People may add their own Webhooks" on or off and sees every Personal Webhook, whose it is and where it points (masked), and can remove one.
