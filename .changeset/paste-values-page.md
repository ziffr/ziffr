Paste values from a spreadsheet: copy cells in Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice or Numbers and paste them on the new **Paste values** page (in the Metrics list), or **Paste history** on a Metric. Ziffr shows what each value would do before anything is recorded, lets you leave values out, and records them with the Metric's usual rules. A Metric's owner, or an Owner or Admin, can record past periods without a value as history.
