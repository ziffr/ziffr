**Gallery**: a page that shows every built-in way to draw a Metric, each drawn with one of your own Metrics (or sample data), so you can see what is possible. Start from a Metric and see every view with it, or start from a view, press Use this…, choose the Metric and add it to a dashboard or a new one. It is under the Dashboards page.
