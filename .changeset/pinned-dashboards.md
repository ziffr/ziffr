Pin the dashboards you open every day: **Pin** on a dashboard or on its card in the Dashboards list puts it in the sidebar under Dashboards (up to eight, yours alone). Drag to reorder, or use Move up and Move down in its ⋯ menu; the shrunk sidebar shows them as tiles, and on a phone they head More. A dashboard that is no longer shared with you leaves your pins quietly.
