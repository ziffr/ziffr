New Recipe on Analyze: "Did it work?". Name a date and Ziffr compares the periods after it with where the Metric's own trend pointed, as a range ("about 12 % above, likely between 8 % and 16 %"), with a Metric the change should not have touched set beside it if you name one. A Metric's change in level or trend can also be searched for without knowing the date: Ziffr names the date that fits best, how it went before and since, or says nothing stands out.
