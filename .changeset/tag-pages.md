Tags on the screens. A Metric's Settings tab has a Tags row where its owner and Editors type words to tag it, and its Tags show as chips on the page and in the Metrics list. The Metrics list has a Tag filter: pick one Tag or several to see the Metrics that carry all of them, and bookmark or share the filtered list. Owners and Admins rename, merge and delete Tags under Workspace → Tags; Members see the list. You see a Tag only through a Metric you may see.
