A ready n8n workflow that fills Metrics from a Microsoft Excel table on OneDrive or SharePoint: one row per value (the Metric's slug, a number, optionally the period as a date), read every morning and sent through a Feed. A Pack's `feeds` may name `excel`. Its guide, `docs/automations/excel-feed.md`, also says how to send from Microsoft 365 without n8n.
