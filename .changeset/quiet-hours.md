Quiet hours: you can choose the hours of the night and the days (such as the weekend) when Ziffr sends you no request for numbers, reminder or Alert, in your own time zone (Your Deliveries). Owners and Admins can set a default for the Workspace. Messages that fall inside wait and are sent when the quiet hours end; none is dropped, and no Due Date moves.
