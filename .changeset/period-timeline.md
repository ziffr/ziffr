The Workflows page now shows what happened in each period of a Workflow, in order: who was asked and reminded, which values came in ("5 of 6 in"), Approvals, what locked and the Snapshots made, showing each person only what they may see. Assistants can ask "How is the weekly close going?" with the new `period_status` tool, and a Workflow can tell its owner when a period locks.
