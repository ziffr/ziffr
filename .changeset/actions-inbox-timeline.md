The Actions page has two views: an Inbox that groups your Questions and Tasks into what needs you, what waits on others and what is done (folded, grey and never red), with the value and its change on each row, and a Timeline of what happened, newest first, by day, that you can narrow by Workflow, Metric and period. Closed Actions are no longer shown in red.
