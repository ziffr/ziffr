The Schedule and the Actions page are now one page, Schedule, with three views: To do (values to submit, Approvals, Tasks and Questions waiting for you, overdue first), Asked by me (Questions and Tasks you asked, answered ones first, closed ones folded, grey and ticked, never red) and a Timeline of what happened, newest first, that you can narrow by Workflow, Metric and period. The old Actions entry is gone from the sidebar and its address opens Asked by me. In a dashboard's overview, a sparse dashboard on a big screen now fits more tiles per row instead of stretching them.
