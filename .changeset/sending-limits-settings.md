Self-hosters: the daily and hourly ceilings on emails, chat messages, Alerts, values, calls, Webhooks, sign-in links, Contacts and Snapshots can now be moved within a range, in a new "Sending limits" section of the Installation settings page or with `ZIFFR_LIMIT_*` variables. None can be switched off, and lowering one never deletes or stops what exists.
