Analyze now has one way to run an analysis: Choose a method, open on an empty canvas and starting with what fits the Metrics on it. The tiles and Recipe chips are gone. The questions use your Metrics' own names ("What moves Cost?"). A Metric's Analysis tab now shows what Ziffr already knows about it (the forecast with its chart first, then what fits of the usual range, the Target outlook and when it changed), the five Metrics that move most like it, and a way into Choose a method with that Metric chosen; a chart whose axis is stretched by a few far points says so. The Metric to explain is marked on the canvas, kept with it and independent of the order of the Metrics, and a Takeaway always shows its sentence before its cautions. Self-hosters: this adds one column to the Analysis Canvas table.
