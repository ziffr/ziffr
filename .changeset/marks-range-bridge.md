View Designs can draw a waterfall and a range. A **bridge** mark shows what changed as a start, each part going up or down and an end, from the new `bridge` step of an Analysis Recipe (a part that went down hangs down, is hatched and is written with its minus; a part with no value in one of the two periods is named). A **range** mark shows each driver's effect from the new `effects` step as an interval, "likely between" its low and high end, and in a chart shows a forecast's likely range at each period. Every number is written in your Number Style, in the Widget, a Snapshot, a View Link and a deck, and a table that cannot be drawn honestly is named as not drawn. The format is described in the View Designs and Analysis Recipes guides.
