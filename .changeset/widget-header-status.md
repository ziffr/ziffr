A dashboard widget's title bar is tidier: the open questions and tasks show as a speech bubble with just the number (no "open"), and a number or gauge widget's status (On target, Too low, Too high) sits beside the title when the card is wide enough, instead of under the value, so the gauge no longer has a pill squeezed beneath it. In a narrower card the status stays under the value, as before.
