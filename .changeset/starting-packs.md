Five starting Packs come with Ziffr, in the `packs` folder: SaaS metrics, Online shop, Agency or freelancer, Sports club, and a Board meeting deck with its Theme. Install one on the Packs page to start from Metric Catalog entries, dashboards, Workflow and Deck Templates made for that kind of work. Two Packs may now describe the same Metric; the catalog lists both.
