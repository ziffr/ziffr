A Workspace's Owner can now download a Workspace Export from Settings: the Metrics and their values, dashboards, decks, Workflows, Themes, Groups, Shares and people as invitations, in one file that is made while it downloads and never stored. It asks for a sign-in from the last 10 minutes, says what stays out, never holds a key or secret, and is in the Audit log. The ended screen of a trial offers it too.
