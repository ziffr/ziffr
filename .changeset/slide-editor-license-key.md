Decks and free layouts need a license key from tldraw to show on an `https://` address; without one they disappeared after a few seconds. **If you run Ziffr:** get a key for your domain at https://tldraw.dev/pricing (a 100-day free trial, commercial and hobby licenses) and enter it on the Installation settings page under **Slide editor**, or set `ZIFFR_TLDRAW_LICENSE_KEY` on the server. Until then a notice stands in their place.
