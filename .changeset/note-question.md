When only a number comes in, Ziffr now asks once, inside the confirmation you get anyway, whether anything is behind it: by email, Slack, Telegram and under the forms, with **Enter all** on Slack for several numbers at once. Each Metric says when it asks under **Ask for a note** (always, which is the default, only when off target or unusual, or never), also for several Metrics at once and through the API.
