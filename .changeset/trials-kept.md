Self-hosters: with `ZIFFR_TRIAL_THEN=keep`, a Trial Workspace becomes an ordinary Workspace on the default Workspace Limit at its end instead of being deleted: invitations open, every Connector reaches everyone and keys keep their own end dates. `ZIFFR_ONE_DEFAULT_PER_PERSON=1` allows one Workspace on the default limit per person. Ziffr Cloud's `endTrial` ends a trial at once when an Owner books a Size.
