Text boxes on slides can show live values: choose **Insert value**, pick a Metric and what to show, and it is filled each time the slide is shown, for what each person may see. Slides made from a template or layout now stay current, and saving one leaves out numbers typed into text boxes unless you keep them; older decks keep their words.

Self-hosters: `scripts/find-typed-figures.sql` lists decks and templates whose text boxes hold typed numbers; it only reads.
