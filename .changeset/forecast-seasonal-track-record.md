Forecasts now use the yearly pattern of a Metric when it has two seasons of values and doing so would have missed less on the last third of its history, and say which Method was used. A forecast's Takeaway gives the expected value with the range it is likely between, how the Method did on the periods already known, and, for a Metric with a Target, how likely it is to be reached ("about 7 in 10"). New Recipe on Analyze: "Will we reach the Target?".
