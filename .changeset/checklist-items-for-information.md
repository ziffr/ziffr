A Workflow can now send a checklist item: a short text of your own, such as "Invoices must be in by Friday", that goes once for each period to the people and Groups you name, a chosen number of working days after the period ends. It waits out Quiet Hours, holds only your words and a link, never reaches a Contact, and each person can stop it with "Stop telling me". Workflow Templates and Packs carry the items' words, and the Workflow's page, its timeline, the API and the MCP Server show them.
