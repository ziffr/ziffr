A Prompt can now go by several ways at once. Under Your Deliveries and Who gets what, where, pick up to three ways for Prompts, such as Slack, Telegram and email. The first answer counts: the other messages are updated ("Answered on Telegram: 3"), an email's form link says "Already answered", and a later answer follows the Metric's Submission Rule. Reminders go by your first way only.
