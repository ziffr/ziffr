Programs can now share what you own, manage Groups and invite or remove people through the API and the SDK, with three new Access Key scopes: Share and answer access requests, Manage groups, and Invite and remove people. They are never ticked by default, follow your role (Groups and people need an Owner or Admin), and only a key you make yourself can hold them: assistants and the MCP Server cannot use them.
