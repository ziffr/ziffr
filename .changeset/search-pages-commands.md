Search (⌘K) now finds pages, people, Groups and Workflows as well as Metrics, dashboards and decks: "invite", "webhook" or "approval" lead to the right page, and it offers commands such as New metric, Invite someone, Record a value for a Metric you submit, Switch Workspace and Toggle sidebar. Opened empty, it lists your recent items and the commands. Everyone is only offered what their role can open, and Guests find nobody but themselves.
