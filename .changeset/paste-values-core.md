Developers: the core previews and records a paste from a spreadsheet (`previewValues`, `recordPastedValues`) through the web form and the person's own Tokens, with the Submission Rule, Locks, the Unusual Value check and Approvals. "Record as history" makes it an Import, only for the Metric Owner or an Owner or Admin. A paste holds at most 2,000 values, and a person pastes at most 10,000 a day across their Workspaces. The intake log now marks pasted values and links each recorded value.
