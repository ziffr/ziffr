Save a whole deck as a Deck Template and a Workflow as a Workflow Template, and start new decks and Workflows from them: the Metrics of a deck become Slots to fill, and a Workflow Template keeps its steps but not its people, so Approvers are picked each time. Developers: their file formats, `ziffr.deck-template/1` and `ziffr.workflow-template/1`, are in `docs/reference/` and can go in a Pack.
