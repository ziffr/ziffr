Decks have Slide Elements: headlines, figures, column charts, gap and metric tables, lists, a logo place and cover art that follow the deck's Theme and fill their words from today's data. Add them with **Element** in the deck editor; a slide can be a cover, and a Theme gained colours for them. Deck Templates and Themes that use these are written as `ziffr.deck-template/2` and `ziffr.theme/2`.
