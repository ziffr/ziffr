A trial can now invite colleagues by email, once its Owner has recorded a first value of their own: up to 5 people, 10 invitation emails a day and one open invitation per address. Owners and Admins see what the trial has used on the trial bar and on Try it. Self-hosters: the three new Trial Limits (people, invitation emails a trial and all trials send a day) are listed in the self-hosting guide.
