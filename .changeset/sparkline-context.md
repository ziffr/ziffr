A trend line or bars under a number now show their context once the Widget has room (Overview and One at a time when presenting, or a large Widget): the first and last period, a dot on the latest value, the highest and lowest value, and the target as a faint band or line. The line's range is fitted to its values, so it no longer looks flat. Widgets of the usual size and phones show the bare trend line as before.
