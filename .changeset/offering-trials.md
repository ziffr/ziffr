Self-hosters: a public Ziffr can offer Trial Workspaces with `ZIFFR_TRIAL_DAYS`: anyone signs in and gets a workspace of their own in which email, Slack, Telegram, the API, AI assistants and Feeds work for real but reach only them, deleted a week after it ends. See "Offering trials" in the self-hosting guide; use an Installation of its own for it.
