Trials now exist only on hosted Ziffr. Self-hosters: without `ZIFFR_CLOUD_KEY` there are no Trial Workspaces, no `ZIFFR_TRIAL_*` settings, no Trials section on the Installation settings page and no trial on the sign-in page; a `ZIFFR_TRIAL_DAYS` that is still set (on the server or saved on the settings page by an older version) stops new Workspaces from being made until you remove it. To let people look around, offer the shared demo.
