Trials now exist only on hosted Ziffr. Self-hosters: without `ZIFFR_CLOUD_KEY` there are no Trial Workspaces, no `ZIFFR_TRIAL_*` settings, no Trials section on the Installation settings page and no trial on the sign-in page; a `ZIFFR_TRIAL_DAYS` that is still set is ignored and Workspaces made here are ordinary ones. To let people look around, offer the shared demo.
