Update an installed Pack from a newer file, or uninstall it, on the Packs page. Ziffr first shows what changes: items nobody changed take the new version, items you changed stay as they are with the new version offered beside them (**Take the new version** keeps yours in Versions), and uninstalling keeps what you changed and Themes in use. Nothing made from a Pack changes.
