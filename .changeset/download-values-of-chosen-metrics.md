You can now download the values of the Metrics you choose. Tick Metrics in the Metrics list and select **Download values** to get one CSV file, or use **Download values** on a Metric's History tab for that Metric alone. It works for everyone who signs in, for the Metrics they may see. A new last column, `state`, says whether each value is final or not approved yet, also in the Workspace's and a trial's download.
