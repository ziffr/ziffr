Two more n8n templates fill Metrics for you: **Stripe** sends monthly recurring revenue, customers and customers who cancelled, and **HubSpot** sends deals won and new contacts, each once a month for the month that has just ended. Get them with **Fill from n8n** on a Metric or after Set up from a Pack, and a Pack's `feeds` may name `stripe` and `hubspot`. They only read from the other system and send nothing but the figures.
