Self-hosters: on a demo-only Ziffr where visitors may change the demo (`ZIFFR_DEMO_CHANGES`, off by default), the line on every demo page also says when the demo starts fresh ("Resets in 9 min."), a page open on a dashboard or deck of the demo's own follows it to its new version after a reset, and a visitor's pins and home dashboard are kept in their browser. With the setting off nothing changes.
