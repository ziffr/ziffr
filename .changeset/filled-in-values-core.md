A Stand-in's answer to a Prompt sent in someone's place now counts as that person's value, marked as entered by the Stand-in, and only for the periods they were asked about. The Metric's owner and the Workflow's owner can fill in a value for a submitter after the Due Date or while they are Away; the submitter is told and can replace it until the period locks, and nobody approves a value they entered. A Guest can no longer be chosen as a Stand-in. Self-hosters: this adds a database migration. Developers: the screens come next, and the API does not fill in for anyone.
