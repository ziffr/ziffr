Your Ziffr dates in your calendar: on **My schedule**, **Get a calendar link** makes a secret address that Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook can subscribe to. It shows the values you must submit, your Approvals and dated Tasks as all-day entries that link into Ziffr. Choose **No names** if others can see your calendar. You can make a new link or turn it off at any time, and Owners and Admins see who has one and can revoke it on the Access Keys page.
