Owners and Admins can now lower some limits for their Workspace (Alerts and Alert messages per person, the longest life of a View Link, the share of daily emails Alerts may use, when a Metric counts as unused), and each person can lower their own Alert messages, overdue look-back and Calendar Link window. Self-hosters: two new Sending Limits, Alerts per person and Alert messages per person per day, set the ceiling.
