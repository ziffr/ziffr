**Set up from this Pack**: under an installed Pack, Owners and Admins pick which of its Metric Catalog entries to make into Metrics (a Derived Metric with the Metrics it is computed from), which of its Dashboard Templates to make into a dashboard with its Slots filled by them, and which Workflow Template to use, with its Approvers. Each Metric counts towards the Workspace's limit; when the set-up does not fit, nothing is made.
