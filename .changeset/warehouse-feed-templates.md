Ready n8n workflows that fill Metrics from BigQuery or Snowflake: a query you write runs once a month for the month that has ended, and the rows it returns are sent through a Feed. A Pack's `feeds` may name `bigquery` or `snowflake`. Their guides are `docs/automations/bigquery-feed.md` and `docs/automations/snowflake-feed.md`.
