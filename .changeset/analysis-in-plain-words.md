Analyze now answers in plain words: every result is a Takeaway with its range, a strength word ("strong" to "can't tell from chance") and one line that says what it does not show. Choose a method lists every Method by the question you have, with when to use it and when not. Ziffr also tells you, without being asked, when two Metrics start or stop moving together, when a Metric turns around, when its Target looks out of reach and when a value is unusual for the time of year. New charts: radar, Formula Tree, Pareto, stacked bars over time and bump. Developers: an Extension Kit lets you add a calculation of your own to the library.
