An assistant connected to Ziffr now gives you the link to a Metric, when you are asked next and who is asked and where, after it creates or changes one or records a value for it, and it no longer stops to ask about channels or settings when you say what you want to track. A refusal it can fix itself (a Metric that does not take values from the assistant, or one you are not asked for yet) comes with the fix, and an odd-looking value is a question, never a refusal.
