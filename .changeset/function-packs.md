Five new starting Packs for the functions of a business: **Sales**, **Finance**, **People and HR**, **Customer support** and **Operations and fulfilment**. Each brings its Metrics (with the usual ratios worked out, such as win rate, gross margin and attrition), a monthly or weekly dashboard and a Workflow that asks for the figures, and names the ready n8n templates (Google Sheets, Excel, HubSpot) that can fill them. Download them from the `packs` folder and install under Workspace → Packs.
