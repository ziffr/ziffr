On the Analysis Canvas, **Choose a method** lists the Methods by what you want to know ("Does A move with B?", "What moves B?", "Will we reach the Target?", "Did it work?"). A Method that does not fit what is on the canvas stays in the list, greyed, with the reason in words. Choosing one asks for its Metrics and settings in plain questions and gives the same Takeaway as its Recipe; the result can be saved as a widget where there is one, and downloaded as an Analysis Recipe for a Pack.
