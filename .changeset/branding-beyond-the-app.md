Your team default Theme now dresses what Ziffr sends and shows outside the app: emails (logo, accent bar and your footer, readable in dark mode, plain text kept), the personal form link, View Link pages and a side bar on Slack messages. Emails always say who really sent them ("<Workspace> via Ziffr" as the sender, and "Sent by Ziffr for …" in the footer); sign-in emails stay unbranded. Logos are limited to 256 KB and 512 × 512 pixels (PNG, JPEG or a plain SVG), and the Themes page previews the email in light and dark colours.
