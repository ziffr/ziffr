The Metric's owner and its Workflow's owner can now fill in a missing value for a submitter from the app: **Fill in for …** on a period's page and in the Metric's Workflow box, open after the Due Date or while the submitter is Away. Every value someone entered for another person says "Entered by Ben for Sara" on the period's page, in the period timeline, in the Metric's history and in the Changes list; a Guest sees only their own name. A Stand-in's form link says whom they answer for.
