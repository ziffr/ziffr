New View, Slope chart: two moments side by side on two vertical axes, one line per row, labelled at both ends, with the largest rise and the largest fall named in words. It compares the rows of a Metric's Breakdown with the period before or a year earlier, or two to twelve Metrics in one unit with their previous period; a row in only one moment is marked new or gone.
