# Words in Ziffr

Every word Ziffr uses, and what it means. The app, this guide and the people who build Ziffr all use these words in the same way, so a word here means the same thing on every screen.

Some entries end with "Elsewhere you may see": words other tools use for something similar. Ziffr does not use them, because they usually mean something slightly different. If you are used to a "KPI", look up Metric.

Throughout the guide, a term in bold leads to its entry here.
